About

joshuanicktan@gmail.comLinkedIn

I’m a mechatronics robotics engineer at Griffin Labs, LionsBot’s embodied-AI research arm in Singapore. I build the hardware for mobile manipulators that clean restrooms (toilets included).

Most recently I led the hardware team on the T1 dual-arm robot: 12 interns over two internship cycles, and three working robots in about eight months. It was shortlisted for the Innovation Award at Interclean Amsterdam. I also designed the setup the AI team used to record its training data, at the same time as the robot itself.

I studied Engineering Product Development (EPD) at SUTD and graduated as EPD’s valedictorian. In my first term I learnt CAD, laser cutting and 3D printing in a month to build a RoboClash robot. It lost its first four matches, then won the finals. I’ve also taught robotics since 2018, and CAD to educators for NWH Holdings.

What I do

Section titled “What I do”
  • Mechanical design, from requirements and hand calculations to FEA: T1’s swerve-drive base, Click&Go’s fail-secure lock and Grip-X’s gripper.
  • Prototyping and fabrication, and getting parts ready to make in batches: Click&Go’s carbon fibre shell, EHD pumps made with a laser cutter and design for manufacture at Fabrica.AI.
  • Power and wiring: T1’s battery swap and power system and Fabrica.AI’s electrical system.
  • Tools with AI that check a design before it’s built: the cable-routing planner, the water-path simulator, the screw-access checker and how I build them.

Experience

Section titled “Experience”
  • Mechatronics robotics engineer, Griffin Labs (LionsBot), Singapore. June 2025 to now. Led the hardware team for the T1 dual-arm robot: three units in about eight months, shortlisted for the Interclean Amsterdam Innovation Award. Between the two, the mechanical architecture for a new LionsBot robot series, not yet announced. Now hardware lead on T1’s single-arm successor.
  • CAD instructor, NWH Holdings (Autodesk Partner), Singapore. November 2022 to May 2025. Beginner and intermediate CAD and fabrication workshops in Fusion 360 and SolidWorks, for educators and students at tertiary institutes.
  • Research assistant, Maeda Lab (Smart Materials and Robotics), Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan. June to August 2024. A way to make wire-electrode EHD pumps for soft robots with only a laser cutter, in 20 minutes and for about S$0.80 each, and a paper I co-authored.
  • Mechanical design and hardware intern, Fabrica.AI, Singapore. August to December 2023. Design for manufacture and assembly on an autonomous grouting robot: 30% fewer fasteners, and drawings for small-batch production that cut its cost by up to 20%.
  • Robotics trainer, Sparklife Asia. November 2018 to December 2022. Workshops and makeathons for 30 to 40 students, and competition mentoring.
  • Testing engineer (intern), LionsBot International. May to August 2019. A sensor and harness test rig for the assembly line.
  • Student researcher, SUTD, with LionsBot. March to May 2018. A reconfigurable cleaning robot.
